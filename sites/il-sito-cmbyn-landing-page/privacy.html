Privacy & note legali

The short version up front: this page collects nothing. No cookies, no tracking, no analytics, no forms. The only thing it ever stores is your theme choice, kept in your own browser.

01 What this site is

Il Sito is an independent, non-commercial study page: a stratigraphic survey of the Lombardy filming places of Call Me by Your Name (2017). It is a single static document. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the film, its makers, or the places it names.

02 No cookies, no tracking, no consent banner

This site sets no cookies and loads no analytics or tracking script. There is nothing to measure, nothing to sell, and no third party reporting back. That is why there is no cookie banner: one would have nothing to consent to.

03 The one thing stored: your theme

Choosing the dark (scavo) or light (rilievo) theme saves a single value, ilsito.theme, to your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device, it identifies nothing, and clearing your site data removes it. In private-browsing mode it may not persist at all.

04 External requests

  • Typography. Three typefaces load from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com). Visiting the page therefore sends your IP address to Google, as with any site that serves web fonts this way. No other Google service is involved.
  • Photography and film. Every plate is licensed stock from Pexels, served locally from this page, and credited on its plate and in the footer with a link to the author's Pexels profile. Those links open only if you click them.
  • Nothing else. All media is bundled into the page itself; there are no ad networks, no CDNs beyond the fonts above, and no server-side requests from the viewer's browser.

05 Intellectual property

  • Call Me by Your Name (2017) and its title, images, and sounds are the property of their respective owners. This page is a fan appreciation that names and describes places; it reproduces no film frames, audio, or substantial excerpts.
  • All photography and clips are Pexels-licensed stock, free to use with attribution, which this page gives per plate. The villa at Moscazzano is private; it is never shown, only described. Where a plate is not the exact location, it is marked Rappresentativo and says so.
  • The page's own design, survey copy, and code are original to this build.

06 No data collection, therefore no retention

There are no forms, no accounts, no comments, and no contact field on this page, so there is no personal data collected, stored, or retained. If you believe something on this page is wrong, or a credit is missing, the cleanest route is to raise it where the page is shared.

07 Changes

This notice describes a one-page static site and will only change if that stops being true. It is dated 20 September 2026.
